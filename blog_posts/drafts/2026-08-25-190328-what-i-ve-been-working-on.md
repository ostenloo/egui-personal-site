This is what I've been working on the past 3 months. 

1) Mirgenta -- an AI Agent System that can listen to music 
2) SWAY -- An affective conversation LLM benchmark (in progress)
3) ROTOM -- Superhuman AI for competitive Pokemon Battling (has nothing to do with LLMs)
4) Leetcode -- need to be able to pass interviews

If I had to describe the nature of this work...

- Mirgenta is the most straightforward. It requires architectural understanding, a little bit of system design, musical domain knowledge, and a little bit of taste for what's important. 
- SWAY is a bit more centered around psychology and statistics. I've honestly been putting off the GRPO fine tuning because it requires tons of hand labels (which I will get to). If I were to be honest, I'm unsure how grounded my reward function for the GRPO fine-tuning is to accurately simulate the engine x delivery core. To be fair though, I think the entire field of utilizing LLMs as patient simulation is not very well fleshed out either, and I do believe my approach has its merits. 
- ROTOM is my most experimental project. Its ideation has been sitting with me since 2022 honestly, and it comes from my experience being a top player in the PokemonShowdown Gen9RandomBattles format. AI assisted coding has honestly made the implementation a lot simpler as quite honestly it requires massive engineering chops to build out the experiment. This is not an LLM based Agent, its a Reinforcement Learning Agent. I know of current search based Agents like Foul Play -- my approach is different, and hopefully it works. 
- LeetCode. In my downtime I do some LeetCode practice. I'm not very good, honestly, but I hope to get better.
