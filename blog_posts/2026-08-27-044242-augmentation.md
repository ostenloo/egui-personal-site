Some people are saddened by AI surpassing expert level performance in their domains. I'm personally not as I was far from an expert in any domain pre-AI, except useless ones like PokemonShowdown and Battle Bears. 

What did strike me as useful was the Augmentation that AI could potentially have on human Software Engineers, including myself. It allows us to build more meaningful and complex projects. 

There's a concept in Cognitive Psychology called "chunking". Basically, its the process of grouping together smaller pieces of information into a meaningful whole, making it easier to recall and apply intuitively. Its how smart people and top performers get stuff done. 

Software Development is becoming similar. We can build Software more easily because the smaller pieces are cheaper to reproduce. The development cost has been abstracted away. So we should adjust accordingly. What capabilities can we gain from AI-generated Software that isn't just churning out more LOC? That requires a bit of creativity. 

I believe the value of Software is being augmented.
